# Assembly of the dry R2K tactile array

Read the [drawings](TA-drawings.pdf) beside the [dimension table](Fabrication.md). Prepare a disconnected board, microscope, flat supported stage, PH0 driver, cross-slide, fine cutter, smooth spring clamps and the purchasing BOM. Record the thickness of actual sheet stock before finishing the aluminum stops. All references below are to the numbered assembly drawing.

## Tool setup and dry fit

1. Deburr all mating surfaces. Check each L12 screw runs freely by hand into each tapped L01 hole, the head seats inside L11 and the tip clears the rear plane. With no wires installed, assemble the measured stock. Use lightly marked bearing surfaces or optical contact to establish all twelve touches at the four L04 shelves, four cap ledges and four grid ledges. Before fitting L06 and the roof, record the top of L04 at the bars against the backing datum with a depth microscope. In a second setup with the cap seated and diaphragm absent, measure the accessible cap top at all nine sites against the same datum. Subtract the measured cap, L08 foil and L07 PI thicknesses to recover the lower liner plane. The on-axis ray through a port reaches the floor copper through the board window. Measure and bound the combined inward deflection of the loose liner and roof on a matching loaded witness, including pressure reversal. Accept 0.540–0.560 board to nominal roof and ≤0.050 inward excursion.
2. Put two identical 0.8 mm winding guide PCBs over the 1.6 mm base. Insert twenty-four deburred Ø0.30×6 mm steel pins through the two sets of Ø0.40 plated holes. Hold them perpendicular in a flat guide comb while shifting the two boards oppositely in x until the pin surfaces are pinched. Tighten the two M2 lock bolts through Ø2.4 round holes. The two lock nuts fit into Ø6 base reliefs. Set guide tops about 0.8 mm above the wire plane. Check each pin stays vertical and does not slide under a light pull. Insert the other two M2 clamp screws through the slots: these secure both plates to the base after lateral positioning. Support the base on feet sufficiently high for the underside hardware.
3. On a flat registration table, finish L07 and L08 as a closely matched, independently perforated pair. Inspect the nine lips from the PI side with transmitted light. Record minimum rim ≥0.050 mm. Hold the pair together with two small smooth spring clips at the +x projecting tab root, one by each side near y=±4.5. Keep their jaws entirely outboard of x=8.55, gripping both sheets before the PI ends at x=8.90. Use thin tips able to reach this ~0.35 mm grip band; witness the alignment at each end. Remove the precise table pins. Clip these two layers only. The other parts slip over the separate temporary screw studs.
4. Make two temporary M1.2 studs from spare 4 mm screws by removing the head and deburring the cut end. For the dry assembly put two studs diagonally into L01 only 0.6–0.8 mm deep. Place L01 on the fixed base and locate it with two diagonally opposite temporary bottom screws through the base, using suitable short engagement or washers. Use separate corners from the studs. The jig’s routed cable slot is under the exposed terminal terrace. Attach ten labeled flexible leads T0…T9 to L04, clean the solder joints and provide off-board strain relief. Check bare PCB continuity, designated paired bars and isolation; allow ≥5 mm cable bend radius and keep bare conductor out of the backing envelope.

## Assembly of copper base and active heating layer

5. Lower L02 onto the backing with its tab at +x, then fit L03 and the L04 carrier. Confirm that the four carrier shelves pass through their reliefs and touch the board underside. Each of the 12 via mouths must have complete lower gasket coverage, including the three x=−6.95 mm sites. Check L02/backing-to-T0…T9 insulation >10 MΩ at low test voltage. Floor and backing can share chassis potential.
6. Give each row four separate 55 mm pieces of AWG48 bare NiCr. Take matching sides of the Ø0.30 guide pairs on each end to obtain centerlines at ±0.626 and ±0.294 mm from row center. For a two-wire row use the outer two. For three use the outer two plus the negative inner lane. Gently take up slack and mechanically hold the ends at the exterior pins. Translate the joined frame pair up to ±0.50 mm in y, inspect all twelve land centers, then tighten the two slotted M2 clamps. Verify 48 crossings in the four-wire build. The lower window is 2.70–3.10 mm along each accepted wire lane. Measure a magnified plan and side profile: cold excess arc length ≤0.010 mm per 4.20 mm bar interval, no kink or strand crossover.
7. Seat the reusable 0.20 mm spring-stainless U-shaped tool. Its 0.25 mm narrow transverse toe sits at x=8.00–8.25 and covers all right wire ends. The two arms at y=±5.40 project toward +x. Apply light downward spring load with two bench clamps beyond the tab edge, while the board supports the toe. Nudge the tails beyond the toe with clean tweezers to confirm they are held. Cut at x=+8.48 against a sacrificial edge under the blade, using the open middle of the U. Remove the right-hand guide offcuts and reinspect. Leave the left ends tensioned and the temporary tool in place. Lower the one-piece L06 over the two studs. Its four ears register with the final hole pattern, its six cross-row bridges preserve the individual ring positions, and gravity plus the studs keep it in place while level.

## Assembly of top diaphragm, aperture and copper layers

8. Lower the tab-clipped L07/L08 pair as a unit, PI down. Keep both clips attached during the transfer. Fit L09 with its four ears over their separate hard backing ledges. With the cap held lightly and L10 absent, inspect all nine bores in reflected light through the windows where visible. Compare both exterior optical witness marks to their initial readings. Place relaxed L10 over the studs, followed by L11. A small relaxed overhang is preferable to stretching the diaphragm at a screw hole.
9. Start two permanent screws in the corners without studs, removing corresponding temporary bottom screws one at a time while holding the stack flat. After they engage, replace both studs individually with permanent screws. Tighten in alternating diagonal increments with a light hand PH0 driver. Stop at complete bearing of the tiered seats. Verify each bearing and compare both tab fiducials under magnification; reject a shift greater than 0.010 mm or the measured remaining rim allowance. If a lip cannot be seen after assembly, the recorded open-stage inspection and paired witness marks are its verification. Remove both tab clips after all seats and alignment marks pass, clearing the withdrawal path of the U-shaped tool. Check for any rise above the islands, tilted screws, thread bottoming or wrinkled silicone. Establish working driver torque on a sacrificial matched assembly or force-sensing witness and record it; avoid using an assumed screw-torque-to-preload conversion.
10. Release the two spring tool clamps. Translate its U toward +x until the toe is beyond x=11.30, then lift before reaching the first guide. It clears the lower board, registered gasket and right-going wires in the installed gap. Cut the remaining left guide tails at x=−7.33. Collect all offcuts, lift the head and check all tool pieces were removed. On a sacrificial contact strip verify that the compressed gasket prevents wire feed across bar supports under axial disturbance and after heating; check cold tap-to-tap stability again on the assembled head.
11. With all ten leads disconnected from electronics, measure both copper sheets to every tap (>10 MΩ). L02 normally contacts L01. Measure L08 to that chassis before adding any intentional bridge. If it conducts, inspect its four screw rims and re-center the upper sheets. Check cold resistances and perform a modest differential-pressure response/decay check on all nine sites. ## Optional copper bridge

The supplied tabs are flat. The exposed inward faces are 1.5934 mm apart. To obtain a compact lap, support the roots 0.60 mm beyond the body, form two opposing 90° bends with a smooth 0.40 mm minimum inside mandrel and stagger the two bend start lines by one foil thickness. Trial-bend scrap first. The outer projection is about 1.25–1.40 mm for bend starts 0.65–0.80 mm beyond the body; remaining material can form a lap or be trimmed. A 10 mm wide copper block can instead be fit to the measured vertical gap, with approximately 0.7–0.9 mm overlap past the insulation and 1.50 mm final projection. L02 and L08 become deliberately common when joined. Use a remote resistance weld or controlled soldering with heat sunk jaws on a coupon to qualify the joint and prevent nearby silicone/PI distortion. Repeat four-terminal bridge continuity, common-copper-to-all-taps insulation, cold electrical checks and leak checks. The machined rear openings preserve access to the copper floor even with the backing installed.
