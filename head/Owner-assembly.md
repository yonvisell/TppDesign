# Building one four-wire tactile array

Use the [drawings](TA-drawings.pdf), [bench template](Lab-templates-1to1.pdf), [part list](BOM.csv) and [fabrication limits](Fabrication.md). Two caps, two rectangular feeler strips, four through screws and four nuts belong in the finished assembly. Two smooth locating pins, two roof-transfer clips, two scrap supports and soft jaws are ordinary removable bench aids. Print the bench template at 100% and measure both 50 mm reference bars with a ruler. Use clean, uncoated sheets. Viewed from the active face, the center of P22 is x = y = 0, +x points toward the upper cooling tab and +y toward the upper wire row.

1. **Prepare the parts.** Remove burrs, wash off cutting debris, inspect both sides of the floor and caps and check the strips on a flat reference. Measure the actual stock. Check that the two opposite registration pins slide through the dry stack without force. Lay the bare plate on supports leaving the four underside nuts accessible. Orient the 1.65 mm cable terrace to the left. Fit the floor gasket and carrier. All twelve Ø0.30 rear via mouths must lie under intact silicone, particularly the three at x = −6.95 where cover is 0.50 nominal. The ten cable terminals remain accessible. Install and label the unpowered cable before placing the wires. Keep solder, exposed strands and strain relief clear of the left copper floor edge.
2. **Record the unpowered references.** Measure the top of the carrier and bars from a fixed exposed part of the PCB terrace used as the common height datum before covering them. Measure the contact gasket and both gap strips. With the lower plates held at their intended compression, record the actual floor-to-board gap at multiple outside edges. A useful dry trial uses the full stack and two spare smooth pins at diagonals. The target floor gasket thickness is 0.218 from 0.254 free. Remove upper sheets for wire installation and retain the two diagonal locators.
3. **Position the wires.** Support a pair of soft jaws or small clips on each side, centered roughly at x = −18.5 and +18.5. Twelve 55 mm lengths leave ample tail. Lay four straight pieces across each row, parallel to x, at the y offsets on the template. Hold each end in soft jaws with just enough preload to straighten it. The temporary leads extend beyond the still-open stack. Inspect twelve individual wires and all 48 gold-bar crossings at y = +4.2, 0, −4.2. Image an accessible trial row from above and the side, then release its temporary grips and look for a change in straightness or movement through the contacts. The independent thermal-clearance analysis specifies its assumed trapped wire length. A visibly kinked, crossing or slack piece is replaced. Use four-terminal (Kelvin) sensing on the accessible unpowered end-to-end spans and save the cold reference. Each measured path includes its lead, trace and contact contributions. This metrology applies at all heater-wire counts. Individual wire/bar junctions are inaccessible.
4. **Fit the gasket and gap strips.** Lower L05 level over the two locators. Its four screw ears and six bridges hold the gasket together. Check the wire positions through the openings. Drop the two L06 strips flat into the separate upper/lower edge pockets, centered at y = ±6.90. Each strip sits on exterior solder mask, clear of contacts and wires. Check both strip ends lie completely inside the pocket. Keep the assembly flat and the wire grips in place.
5. **Gauge the roof pair off the array.** On two close smooth registration pins place the PI against the copper and inspect all nine ports against transmitted light. Require Ø0.425 ±0.010 PI, Ø0.600 ±0.010 copper, center offset ≤0.025 and ≥0.050 mm continuous dielectric lip. Record the minimum lip and two separated alignment-mark positions with a measuring microscope. Two small removable spring clips hold the overlapping 0.60 mm PI tongue and copper tab root, outside x = +8.55 and clear of the cap footprint. Fit narrow clean jaws and confirm neither sheet wrinkles. Lift the secured pair and place it on the two diagonal array locators with PI toward the wires. Verify the two exterior marks retain orientation and location.
6. **Close the upper assembly.** Lay the first FR-4 cap onto the roof. Lay the diaphragm relaxed and add the identical second cap. Start a ¼ in 00-90 screw from above in each free corner and fit its hex nut underneath. Keep both screws loose. Exchange the two temporary pins for the remaining screws one at a time. Use an IP2 driver and a 5/64 in nut tool. Tighten in small diagonal increments. The flat heads and nuts must bear squarely without dimpling the FR-4 or dishing the plate. Avoid imposing an arbitrary torque on the silicone stack. Inspect for continuous strip seating, flat caps and gasket retention at all four sides. Follow both edge marks during tightening. Their relative displacement plus measurement uncertainty must stay inside the initial measured lip surplus above 0.050 mm. Remove the transfer clips after the pair is captured. If its alignment changes beyond that limit, reopen and inspect it directly.
7. **Measure closure.** The floor and diaphragm have no rigid thickness stops. Check compressed land thickness at several exposed edges, centered on the 0.218 and 0.225 mm targets, and verify the central diaphragm is still relaxed. The two strips set a nominal 0.550 board-to-liner gap. For a reusable unpowered trial cut a small sight hole inside each free diaphragm circle in a scrap of the same stock. Keep all diaphragm lands and fastener holes unchanged. With the caps seated, use a focus-height microscope or noncontact displacement gauge referenced to the same exposed PCB terrace. Measure the roof top beside its Ø0.600 bore at every site. Subtract measured copper and PI thicknesses and that site’s recorded PCB rim height. Track the terrace as the clamp settles. Refit the intact diaphragm and reproduce the trial closure. The installed gap must lie in 0.540–0.560 at all sites. Verify, on a trial assembly through the pressure/thermal range, that combined inward roof and PI motion stays ≤0.050. The length bound further requires no wire feeding from neighboring spans. A clamped sacrificial witness can be exercised and re-opened to inspect the contact imprints. If sheet/plate stiffness does not deliver the required geometry together, reselect measured stock or correct flatness before accepting numerical performance predictions.
8. **Release, trim and test.** Release the temporary outside jaws after the contacts capture the wires. Support each offcut. From above the cable terrace trim the left tails at x ≈ −7.65. From the underside of the +x edge reach up beside the flat floor and carrier and flush-cut the right tails at x ≈ +8.58. The upper copper remains 0.55 mm above the wire plane and the PI tongue protects its root. Inspect under magnification, remove all twelve offcuts from each side, and check separation from the roof, rear plate, terminal lands and adjacent rows. The finished nominal wire length is 16.23 mm. The only copper projection is the 10 × 3 mm upper thermal tab, which can remain flat. The rear plate exposes a central cooling surface.
9. **Record the finished unit.** Check the four screws each extend beyond the nut, all nuts are fully engaged, and the mounting/heat-sink arrangement clears projecting tips. With electronics disconnected, verify >10 MΩ from each of T0–T9 to the rear copper and to the roof. Eccentric screws may touch the roof bore, and the rear plate contacts the nuts. If independent thermal hardware requires electrical isolation of the roof, check >10 MΩ roof-to-floor. Otherwise record the chassis continuity state and retain >10 MΩ to the heater network. Record individual cold span resistances, compare before/after release and confirm there are no wire-to-copper faults. Check cold blocked-pressure decay and idle-neighbor response before relying on the modeled pressure. See the [pressure and clearance review](../analysis/seal/Seal-validation.md).

The cable leaves on −x. The 0.410 mm nominal terminal-land clearance to the floor and 0.635 mm head projection above the top cap constrain adjacent mounting hardware. The rear tips extend 2.792 mm from the plate. The two flat compression caps and 00-90 hardware may be taken apart for wire replacement.
