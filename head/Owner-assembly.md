# Assemble the pixel display

The winding base locates the display on four fixed pins. A removable carrier frame holds the wire guides and can slide transversely while the display stays registered. The reference build uses four straight AWG48 Ni80Cr20 wires per row. Two- and three-wire builds use the same count in all three rows. A complete display contains six, nine or twelve wires. Set the controller to the installed count after measuring this display.

## Prepare the winding tool

1. Deburr the bare boards. Stand the base on two equal blocks with at least 12 mm clear below the cable slot. Seat four smooth Ø0.45 × 6 mm steel pins in its Ø0.65 mm holes. Use a sample carrier as the locating master, separated by release film. Hold the pins vertical with 3.5 mm projection above the base and secure them from below with epoxy. Confirm the stack lifts freely after cure.
2. Fit 24 smooth Ø0.30 × 6 mm pins in the Ø0.40 finished plated seats in the guide frame. Six groups serve the three row ends on both sides. Each group contains two pin pairs staggered in x, on lines 0.46 mm to either side of row centre. Cut hardened gauge stock with an abrasive wheel, stone each end and remove particles. Use a flat spacer while setting the crowned ends 1.0 mm above the frame surface. Secure from below. Keep the fillets within the corresponding 5.55 × 2.90 mm base relief window. A frame is reusable.
3. Lower the frame over the base. Install two M2 screws with flat washers through its slots and use nuts below. The clear opening is 22.30 × 16.90 mm. A centred 15.50 mm board has 0.70 mm of room on either short-axis edge. The frame can move ±0.50 mm from centre for adjustment. Slide while the screws are loose. Check actual wire alignment before tightening. The final wire position is within ±0.08 mm of its specified lane.
4. Cut the flat pressing caul in 2–3 mm plastic or aluminum from the fixture drawing. Four Ø1.80 openings clear the datum pins. Keep the underside smooth. Use release film during pressing. Check for rocking and slippage before applying adhesive.

## Cut and laminate the stock

5. Print the two-page cutting template on A4 at 100%. Measure both 50 mm scale bars with calipers. For a blade plotter use the individual 1:1 SVGs with millimetre dimensions. All patterns show the same face view. Keep stock flat and oversized while perforating. Register the four common holes on a jig or smooth backing plate before trimming the outline.
6. Cut the floor and upper foils from 0.127 mm annealed bare C110. Use small shears or score the outside edges on a sacrificial backing. Keep the complete 10.00 mm-wide, 3.00 mm-long tab on each foil. Both occupy y = −5.00 to +5.00 in face view. Punch Ø0.55 registration holes. Punch nine Ø0.600 apertures in the upper foil through a close-fitting die, or microdrill the foil clamped between two sacrificial plates. Stone raised burrs lightly, clean and flatten. Moving a drill unsupported through 0.127 mm stock can tear the foil. The lower foil is continuous under all nine cells.
7. Use plain 25.4 µm polyimide and double-liner 58.4 µm 3M 467MP. Register L02/L03 with their 0.60 mm root tongue. L04 ends at the laminate edge. Laminate L11/L12 as one film. Cut its outer outline and mounting holes first. Retain its port centres uncut if using the registered drill procedure below. Save three 0.60 × 1.48 mm pieces from the same laminated stock for the L06 wire-tail retainers. Cut L14 separately. For L07–L09, laminate 0.1016 mm PI between two adhesive sheets. Kiss-cut both adhesive coats and PI while retaining the common backing. Transfer the six pads together on a second release liner. Inspect all six under magnification.
8. Make the connected gasket and diaphragm (L10 and L17) from measured plain 0.254 mm silicone. Use the two silicone SVGs, a sacrificial coupon and the lab-approved Rayjet silicone settings. Check the actual profiles and the six short joining bridges under magnification. Use a blade or punch for PI and acrylic adhesives. Cut L16 from 9731 differential tape on its liner and identify the silicone-adhesive side before removing either liner.
9. Measure a same-lot spacer coupon after lamination with the intended caul, pressure and dwell. Target installed thickness 0.215–0.235 mm. Select gasket stock measuring 0.244–0.264 mm under repeatable light gauge pressure. Backlight the gasket over the carrier. Each port has a continuous supported seal, the nine lower windows align inside the gasket apertures, and the gasket clears all six spacer pieces. Use these checks to qualify the cutting method before producing several sets.

## Mount the board and wind the wires

10. Square pad T0 is upper left. Label ten 300 mm flexible AWG26 leads T0–T9. Use ≤0.84 mm jacket OD and ≤0.52 mm prepared conductor diameter. Solder from below to the plated terminal terrace, confining each small top fillet to its 0.98 mm land and ≤0.14 mm above the carrier. Leave the far ends free to pass through the jig. Clean and check each bar-to-terminal connection and isolation between distinct nets against the pin map.
11. Register and bond the floor stack to the carrier underside on all four pins. The two adhesive faces bond the PI to the board and lower copper. Centre its +x tab on y = 0. Keep the copper visible in the nine windows. Under magnification verify continuous L04 coverage over all twelve via mouths, particularly the three at x = −6.95 mm where only 0.20 mm nominal edge coverage remains. Feed the loose leads down through the base slot and seat the board face up. Temporarily shield the cable terrace with smooth film ending outside x = −7.50.
12. Cut separate 55 mm pieces of AWG48 wire with sharp dedicated micro-shears. A 40 mm wide tool needs this surplus for an outer turn and tape anchor. Cut 6, 9 or 12 lengths. Lay each wire straight against the corresponding pair on both ends. Turn the surplus at each outer pin and tape the ends to the frame. Use the smallest tension that removes slack. With positive y toward T0, use the following offsets in every row.

| Strands per row | Centres from row centre, mm |
|---:|---|
| 2 | −0.626, +0.626 |
| 3 | −0.626, −0.294, +0.626 |
| 4 | −0.626, −0.294, +0.294, +0.626 |

13. Adjust and lock the guide frame. Verify under magnification that every wire crosses the four 0.35 × 1.65 mm contact bars and clears adjacent routing. Measure the unsupported length along each installed lane at all nine pixels. Accept 2.70–3.10 mm for the modeled build. Rounded corners can shorten an outer span to 2.66 mm at the combined fabrication and alignment limits. A row-centre measurement misses this effect. Before lowering the upper sheet, place one L06 retainer on each row at x = 7.60–8.20 mm, centred in y on its group. Press acrylic-side down over the outer tails onto clean solder mask. Keep all three pads in their matching spacer reliefs. Maintain only light wire tension. Clip the +x tails at x = +8.33 mm over a thin sacrificial backing, remove the surplus and recheck all lanes. These three permanent PI patches retain alignment in the inactive exits. Leave the −x tails on their guides until closure. Lower the L10 gasket onto A/B. Record the installed count; measure cold resistances after closure.

## Close, inspect and test

14. On a backlit XY drill stage, laminate the L11/L12 outer profile to the L13 copper, adhesive toward copper. Match the four datums and the +x root. Centre on each actual Ø0.600 opening under a measuring microscope, clamp the laminate in the same XY position against a fresh sacrificial plastic backer, and bore Ø0.425 through the dielectric only. Use an entry film to support the cutting edge. A 0.420 mm cutter can be used if the measured finished bore is 0.415–0.435 mm. The nine centres are located separately on the bonded part. Inspect each bore as described below. Bond the L15 aperture cap using L14. Locate the six L07–L09 spacer pieces on C/D against the exposed L11 PI face. Retain their board-facing liners until closure.
15. Remove the spacer liners, then lower the upper assembly over the four pins without dragging the gasket or pretrimmed wires. Press with the flat caul, following the tape supplier's preparation, wet-out and dwell guidance. Leave the two tabs flat and separated with a clean removable insulating slip. Disconnect the driver and verify >10 MΩ from L01 to each of T0–T9, L13 to each of T0–T9, and L01 to L13. Record the readings.
16. When the spacer bonds hold the seal, cut the remaining −x wire tails at x ≈ −7.33 mm using a guard under the shears. The retained wires are 15.66 mm long. Apply the acrylic side of L16 to L15 and its silicone adhesive to L17, leaving the diaphragm relaxed and all nine apertures free. For an unbridged tactile array keep the exposed tabs individually insulated. For the optional thermal connection, remove the temporary slip, support the tab roots and make the selected joint on the external copper only. Check low and stable four-terminal continuity between the lower and upper sheets and >10 MΩ from the joined copper to **each** T0–T9 with the driver disconnected. Record before/after cold resistances, compare membrane response and repeat the leak check. A low interfoil resistance after joining is intended.
17. Lift the display squarely off the pins and feed the leads back through the slot. Keep a ≥5 mm centreline bend radius. Anchor cable jackets to the mounting structure 10–20 mm from the joints. A 1 mm straight drop below the board, 5 mm bend radius and 0.84 mm jacket require 6.42 mm below the carrier underside and about 8.50 mm total from diaphragm to the furthest jacket. The free cable extends further in x.
18. With all unused taps open, measure the nine adjacent terminal resistances and end-to-end resistance. Compare against the actual wire lot, wire count, harness and accepted electrical budget. A changing reading under gentle cable motion or after gasket creep requires diagnosis before thermal operation. Commission with one low-duty isolated pixel, then repeat all nine. With the driver off, use one instrumented sacrificial tactile array to pressurize each cell separately. Record pressure decay and neighbouring-cell response at the intended pressure, before and after forming any bridge. Allow pressure/temperature to settle and account for the measurement-line volume. Check the supporting floor and both face bonds for lift throughout. Then measure displacement under load, face and adhesive temperatures and resistance drift through representative cycles.

## Inspect the nine paired bores

Measure minimum and maximum diameters and the full circumference of each dielectric lip after final lamination. The drawing sizes are Ø0.600 ±0.010 mm for copper and Ø0.425 ±0.010 mm for the dielectric. Relative centre offset ≤0.025 mm gives at least 0.0525 mm calculated lip at these diameter limits. Require ≥0.050 mm observed minimum lip. For example, with ±0.005 mm optical uncertainty, accept a ≥0.055 mm indicated lip to retain the 0.050 mm lower bound. Reject tears, adhesive smear, burrs or a missing sector. Registration pins with 0.10 mm diametral clearance cannot guarantee this overlap. The printed outlines locate rough blanks; the bonded bore and measuring microscope establish final acceptance.
