# Matched clamp hardware RFQ

Quote two finished L01 backings in bare 6061-T6 and two L11 face grids in 304 stainless. The STEP models give the nominal finished shape; TA-drawings.pdf sheet 7 defines the machining and inspection notes. Finish heights only after receiving the measured carrier, copper, upper dielectric and cap thicknesses. Each supplied L01 STEP has four smooth Ø1.20 thread-major envelopes. Drill and tap to M1.2×0.25-6H **through**, inspect for full thread, and keep the entry edge break ≤0.08. Tap drill is selected by the machinist for the chosen cutting or forming process. The floor is recessed 2.000±0.030 from the rear datum. All nine rear cooling openings are through and 2.90×2.00 R0.40.

Measure each of four lands at each elevation from the copper-bearing face. Nominal levels +0.3450, +1.8474 and +2.4724 mm; use measured layer corrections from the manufacturing note. Aim for ≤0.010 mm variation within each set and ±0.010 relative height (micromilling plus lapping as needed). L11 field thickness 0.350±0.015, islands 0.800±0.015. Four flat-bottom Ø2.00 +0.03/−0 holes, 0.580±0.015 deep; Ø1.40 +0.04/−0 through. Each island has Ø2.40 OD. Maintain continuous annular head seats. Flatness 0.020 across field, smooth and hand-polish all exposed skin edges. Confirm capability, manual setup, cost and measured inspection record. A grid that bows past the installed roof/liner clearance limit requires correction before use.
